## Deliberately excluded

- Automated tests and generated test outputs.
- Native AVEVA move evidence and general fixtures.
- Historical stage/version notes.
- POR configuration and Porinio source data.
- Saved recipe archives and personal working copies.
- Legacy/unreferenced sources (`lane (orig).js`, `NewFile`).

## Runtime ownership

- `index.html`: sole browser load manifest.
- `css/viewer.css`: viewer/lane and drag-target visibility styling.
- `js/global-movement.js`: movement interaction and currently unaccepted structural dispatcher.
- `js/editor.js`: Transition-to-Fork drop rendering.
- `js/nested-branch-render.js`: post-Join and branch-to-Join drop rendering.
- `js/xml-authority.js` and `js/exporter.js`: XML authority and structural save/reparse.

No hidden version-specific runtime overlays are included.
